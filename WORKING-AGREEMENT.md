### How we work together

**The quote.** Every project is quoted before it starts — what I am
shooting, what you get back, and when. Video editing runs on fixed
packages: an edit costs $100, in blocks of ten or twenty. Event coverage,
photography, and second shooting are quoted per project.

**Booking the date.** A ____% deposit holds your date. Until the deposit is
in, the date is available to anyone else. The balance is due before the
final files are released.

**Before the shoot.** You get a short written brief — the goal, who is
involved, locations, timings, and what the content is for. It exists so the
shoot day runs on a plan instead of on guesses. If the brief needs to
change, tell me before the day rather than during it.

**Photo and video on the same day.** One camera operator cannot cover both.
If you need stills and video from the same event, the quote includes a
second shooter — that is a real cost, and quoting it up front is how you
avoid a day where half the coverage does not exist.

**Behind-the-scenes film.** Available as an add-on and quoted as its own
deliverable. Good for brand transparency; not free, because it needs a
person whose whole job that day is filming it.

### Turnaround

The clock starts when I have the footage and the brief is agreed — not
from the moment the event ends.

| Deliverable | Delivered within |
|---|---|
| Event video recap | 48–72 hours |
| Photo gallery | 72 hours |
| Edited video from supplied footage | Same week |

If something is going to be late, you will hear it from me first, with a
new date — not silence.

### Revisions

**____ rounds of revisions are included** with every deliverable.

A **revision** is notes on the cut you have: timing, music, captions,
colour tweaks, swapping a shot for another one already filmed, trimming,
reordering.

A **new edit** is a change of direction — different footage, a different
story, a different length or format than the one agreed, or changes made
after you have approved the cut. That is billed as a new edit at $100.

Two things that keep this painless for both of us:

- **Send notes in one message per round.** Notes arriving one at a time
  over three days is three rounds of work, and it will get counted that
  way. Collect them, send them together.
- **Notes within ____ days of delivery.** After that the cut is approved
  and the project closes. You can always come back later — it is simply a
  new edit.

Revisions come back within two business days of receiving your notes.

### Files, rights, and what happens after

**What you get.** The finished cut in the formats agreed — typically a
horizontal master and vertical versions from the same edit — plus the
edited stills gallery where photography is part of the booking.

**Raw footage.** ____________________

**Usage.** You own the right to use the delivered work for your marketing:
paid ads, organic social, your website, in-store. Reselling the footage to
a third party, or handing it to another editor to re-cut, is a separate
conversation.

**My usage.** I keep the right to show the finished work in my portfolio
and on my channels. If something is under embargo or commercially
sensitive, tell me and it stays offline — just say so before delivery.

**Storage.** Project files are kept for 90 days after final delivery. After
that, do not assume anything is recoverable. Download your files.
